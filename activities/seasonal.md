# 🌞 Seasonal

## Summer

### Lollapalooza

Lollapalooza is a major US music festival that takes place annually in downtown Chicago's Grant Park.&#x20;

Timeframe: Late July/Early August

Location: Loop

Budget: $125 for a one day ticket, or about $400 for the whole weekend.

## Winter

### Christkindl Market

Christkindl Market is a Christmas market where artisans open up booths and sell tree ornaments, food, etc.&#x20;

Timeframe: Late November - Late December

Location(s): Loop, Wrigleyville, Aurora (suburb)

Budget: Admission is free, but it is a market. I'd recommend $20 in spending cash per person.&#x20;

{% embed url="https://www.christkindlmarket.com/" %}

![Booth from 2021](<../.gitbook/assets/Screen Shot 2022-08-14 at 9.04.45 PM.png>)

### Ice Skating at the Ribbon

Maggie Daley Park is a public park in the city. Durring winter, they open the Ribbon portion as an outdoor ice skating path.&#x20;

Timeframe: November - March

Location: Loop

Budget: $16 - $20 per person for skate rentals.

{% embed url="https://maggiedaleypark.com/things-to-do-see/skating-ribbon/" %}
