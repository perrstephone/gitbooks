# 💎 Hidden Gems

## Alchemy Arts

Alchemy Arts is a metaphysical occult store in the Edgewater neighborhood of Chicago. This store sells books and accoutrement for various alternative faiths. This store stocks tarot cards, ritual candles, crystals, and more. The store is guarded by Marie, a black cat.

Location: Edgewater

## The Last Chapter Bookstore

The Last Chapter Bookstore is a romance-only bookstore in Chicago's Roscoe Village neighborhood. In addition to their cozy, welcoming atmosphere, the store regularly hosts book signing/Q\&A events with romance authors. If books aren't your thing, this store also stocks candles and cozy Taylor Swift merch. :star:

Location: Roscoe Village

{% embed url="https://thelastchapterbookshop.com/" %}

## AIR Aerial Fitness

If you'd like to workout during your stay, I recommend trying out aerial gyms. AIR is a great gym that specializes in aerial hammocks. The classes range from traditional conditioning in their "foundations" courses to yoga using the fabrics in their "flow" courses.

Budget: $25 for unlimited one week pass
