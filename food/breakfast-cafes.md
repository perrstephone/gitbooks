# 🥞 Breakfast/Cafes

## Move Along Coffee

Move Along is a walk-up, take-away cafe in the West Town area. This cafe serves coffee and teas in addition to select baked goods. The building is decorated with a stunning skeleton mural. This is a great place for getting a coffee while walking a dog.

Location: West Town

Budget: $5-$10

{% embed url="https://www.movealongcoffee.com/" %}

## Do-Rite Donuts

Do-Rite is a local Chicago donut chain. They serve excellent donuts and breakfast sandwiches as well as local coffee from Dark Matter Coffee. Excellent for on-the-go breakfast. I recommend ordering online to save time. It can have a bit of a line at peak times. :star::fire:

Location(s): Loop, Streeterville, West Loop, Wrigleyville

Budget: $10

{% embed url="https://www.doritedonuts.com/" %}

## Stan's Donuts

Stan's Donuts is another local donut chain. They have very cute donuts, and a more cohesive aesthetic.&#x20;

Location(s): Loop, West Loop, South Loop, Streeterville, Wicker Park, Lakeview

Budget: $10-$20

{% embed url="https://www.stansdonuts.com/" %}

## Cafe L'Appetito

A small Italian cafe/store/bakery that serves a variety of pastries and fresh made sandwiches. In addition to traditional Italian cafe fare, this store offers a selection of Italian grocery store items, so it's a great place to pick up little candies, beverages, and cookies. There is a small private outdoor seating area next to the exit of the cafe.&#x20;

Location: River North

Budget: $10-$15

{% embed url="https://lappetito.com/" %}

## Egg Harbor Cafe

Egg Harbor is a sit-down breakfast chain. This is one of the more reasonably priced resturants that exists downtown, especially considering that the food is good and the resturant has a nice atomosphere. :fire:

Location(s): Streeterville, more in the suburbs

Budget: $20-$30

{% embed url="https://eggharborcafe.com/" %}

## Avec

Avec is a pair of Mediterranean restaurants in the downtown area of Chicago. We've only had brunch at Avec, but we loved it. The food is delicious, savory, and refined, making for an excellent upscale brunch spot.&#x20;

Location: West Loop, River North

Budget: $20-$30 (for breakfast/brunch)

{% embed url="https://www.avecrestaurant.com/" %}
