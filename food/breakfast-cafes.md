# 🥞 Breakfast/Cafes

## Coffee Run

### Move Along Coffee

Move Along is a walk-up, take-away cafe in the West Town area. This cafe serves coffee and teas in addition to select baked goods. The building is decorated with a stunning skeleton mural. This is a great place for getting a coffee while walking a dog.

Food: Coffee, Pastries

Location: West Town

Budget: $

{% embed url="https://www.movealongcoffee.com/" %}

### Do-Rite Donuts

Do-Rite is a local Chicago donut chain. They serve excellent donuts and breakfast sandwiches as well as local coffee from Dark Matter Coffee. Excellent for on-the-go breakfast. I recommend ordering online to save time. It can have a bit of a line at peak times. :star::fire:

Food: Coffee, Donuts, Chicken

Location(s): Loop, Streeterville, West Loop, Wrigleyville

Budget: $

{% embed url="https://www.doritedonuts.com/" %}

### Stan's Donuts

Stan's Donuts is another local donut chain. They have very cute donuts, and a more cohesive aesthetic. We find the quality can be hit-or-miss.&#x20;

Food: Coffee, Donuts

Location(s): Loop, West Loop, South Loop, Streeterville, Wicker Park, Lakeview

Budget: $

{% embed url="https://www.stansdonuts.com/" %}

### Voodoo Doughnuts

Voodoo Donuts is a national chain with a location in the heart of the Chicago food district. They offer creative donuts with fun designs.&#x20;

Food: Coffee, Donuts

Location: West Loop

Budget: \$$

{% embed url="https://www.voodoodoughnut.com/" %}

## Bakeries

### Publican Quality Bread

Publican Quality Bread is a local bakery with some of the most delicious breads in Chicago. Their sourdough is one of our absolute favorites. :star::fire:

Food: Pastries, Bread, Coffee

Location: West Town

Budget: \$$

Rewards: [One Off Hospitality](../misc/referral-programs.md#one-off-hospitality)

{% embed url="https://www.publicanqualitybread.com/" %}

### Kasama

Kasama is a Filipino bakery and breakfast spot. Our favorite way to get Kasama is to order at 9am sharp for pickup, and we recommend the cardamom sticky bun. :star:

Food: Filipino, Breakfast

Location: West Town

Budget: \$$

{% embed url="https://www.kasamachicago.com/" %}

### Cafe L'Appetito

A small Italian cafe/store/bakery that serves a variety of pastries and fresh made sandwiches. In addition to traditional Italian cafe fare, this store offers a selection of Italian grocery store items, so it's a great place to pick up little candies, beverages, and cookies. There is a small private outdoor seating area next to the exit of the cafe.&#x20;

Food: Grocer, Italian

Location: River North

Budget: $

{% embed url="https://lappetito.com/" %}

## Sitdown Brunch

### Egg Harbor Cafe

Egg Harbor is a sit-down breakfast chain. This is one of the more reasonably priced restaurants that exists downtown, especially considering that the food is good and the restaurant has a nice atmosphere. :fire:

Food: Brunch, Coffee

Location(s): Streeterville, more in the suburbs

Budget: \$$

{% embed url="https://eggharborcafe.com/" %}

### Avec

Avec is a pair of Mediterranean restaurants in the downtown area of Chicago. The food is delicious, savory, and refined, making for an excellent upscale brunch spot.&#x20;

Food: Brunch, Coffee, Mediterranean

Location: West Loop, River North

Budget: \$$$

{% embed url="https://www.avecrestaurant.com/" %}

### Cafe Robey

Cafe Robey is a chic all-day bunch spot nestled within the Robey Hotel, an independent hotel in the heart of Wicker Park. This classic brunch spot boasts a long list of cocktails and breakfast food, ranging from sweet to savory. Reservations are a good call for weekends.&#x20;

Food: Brunch, Coffee

Location: Wicker Park

Budget: \$$

{% embed url="https://www.caferobey.com/" %}
