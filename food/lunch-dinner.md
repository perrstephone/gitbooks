# 🍔 Lunch/Dinner

## Billy Goat Tavern

Billy Goat Tavern is the tavern famous for the "CHEESBORGER" SNL skit and the Billy Goat Cubs Curse ([https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curse\_of\_the\_Billy\_Goat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curse\_of\_the\_Billy\_Goat)). This iconic Chicago location literally is underground as it lives under Michigan Ave. There are multiple locations, but I recommend the original. It's a great place if you want to enjoy a cheeseburger in a historic resturant.

Location: Streeterville (original), Loop, Wrigleyville, Navy Pier

Budget: $7 for a burger and $3 for a Coke, so approximately $10 for a meal per person.

{% embed url="https://www.billygoattavern.com/" %}

## Portillo’s

Portillo's is a fast food chain resturant that serves traditional Chicago delicacies, such as Italian beef sandwich, Chicago-style hotdog, and a chocolate cake shake.&#x20;

Location: River North

Budget: $5 for a hot dog, $5 for cheese fries, and $3 for a pop, so approximately $13 for a meal per person.&#x20;

{% embed url="https://www.portillos.com/index.html" %}

## French Market

The French Market is a year-round market with several vendors that sell various cuisines. They have Cuban food, Boba tea, a bakery, and more. This location is especially good for getting lunch for an indecisive crowd with diverse tastes. It also provides quick access to a few hubs for transit (Ogilvie train station, for example). Note that the French Market is typically only open for lunch.

Location: West Loop

Budget: Expect to spend $10-$15 for a typical entree.&#x20;

{% embed url="https://frenchmarketchicago.com/" %}

## Cafecito&#x20;

Cafecito is a local Cuban chain resturant. Great sandwiches for a reasonable price.&#x20;

Location(s): Loop, South Loop, Streeterville, Lincoln Park&#x20;

Budget: $10 for a standard Cubano.

{% embed url="http://chi.iheartcafecito.com/" %}

## Nando’s

Nando's is a South African/Portugese chain resturant. There are a lot of these in Europe, but Chicago and DC are also a big US hubs for the chain. They are famous for their peri-peri chicken, but they have non-spicy options as well.&#x20;

Location(s): Streeterville, Loop, South Loop, Old Town, Lakeview, etc.

Budget: $13 for a chicken sandwich and a side.

{% embed url="https://www.nandosperiperi.com/" %}

## Monk’s Pub

Monk's Pub is a pub with reasonably priced food. The interior is decorated to look like it's from a very old-fashioned English town pub.&#x20;

Location: Loop

Budget: $15 for a standard burger.&#x20;

{% embed url="https://www.monkspubchicago.com/" %}

## Untitled Supper Club

This is a very cool venue. This is a revival speakeasy restaurant. The door is unmarked, so make sure you follow Google Maps to a T. They have lots of shows ranging from magicians to jazz to burlesque. Please note this is a 21+ establishment, so plan accordingly. There is also an expectation of business casual dress. :star:

Location: River North

Budget: Burger is $19 and a cocktail is $16, so expect to spend around $42 per person with tip.&#x20;

{% embed url="https://untitledsupperclub.com/" %}
