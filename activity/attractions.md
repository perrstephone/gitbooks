# 🎡 Attractions

## The Bean

You know what The Bean is, it's official name is Cloud Gate. It's located in Millennium Park, which is just north of Grant Park.&#x20;

Location: Loop

Budget: Free

## Grant Park

Beautiful public park located in downtown Chicago! Grant Park is a beautiful area to walk around and take in gorgeous sites like Buckingham Fountain. It's conviently next to the lake, so you can visit both at once.&#x20;

Location: Loop

Budget: Free

## Chinatown

Chinatown is a beautiful neighborhood in the southwest portion of Chicago. This neighborhood is home to a market, several Chinese restaurants (such as Micheli- reviewed dimsum restaurant MingHin), and a few boba vendors.&#x20;

Location: Chinatown

Budget: $0+, technically free to be there, but you should bring spending money.

## Navy Pier

Navy Pier is a tourism attraction that provides lakefront views, resturants, and shopping opportunities. There are vendors selling handmade goods and food. There are multiple rides, such as the famous Chicago Ferris Wheel. Margaritaville, Giordano's, and Harry Carey's are among the resturants that line the pier.

Location: Navy Pier

Budget: $0+, entry is free but you may want some money to spend on a food and souvenirs. The ferris wheel costs $18 per person.

{% embed url="https://navypier.org/plan-your-visit/buy-tickets/" %}

## Lakefront/Riverfront

The Lakefront and the Riverfront offer free walking paths for enjoying the waterways of the city. The Lakefront is particularly good for renting a public bike and riding along the path. The Riverfront is excellent for leisurely strolls. Both provide some small outdoor resturants and bars for some cocktails and snacks.&#x20;

Location: Loop/Streeterville, Lake

Budget: $0+, entry is free but you may want some money for snacks/cocktail.

## Sears Tower

The Sears/Willis Tower is the tallest building in Chicago. The top of the tower has an observatory as well as the "Ledge". The Ledge is a glass box that extends off the the side of the tower, providing a full view of what Chicago looks like at over 1,300 feet in the air.&#x20;

Location: Loop

Budget: $30

{% embed url="https://theskydeck.com/" %}

## 360 Observation Deck&#x20;

This is an observation deck on the 94th floor of the John Hancock Center offering an incredible view of the entire city. It also has a bar and a ride called TILT which is a window that you hold onto as it physically rotates 30 degrees out the building.&#x20;

Location: Streeterville

Budget: $30-$35 per person for just admission, $49-$55 for a ticket that includes admission, TILT, and a drink ticket.&#x20;

{% embed url="https://360chicago.com/" %}

## The Escape Game

The Escape Game is an exciting escape room with high production value in downtown Chicago. :star:

Location: River North

Budget: $40 per person.
