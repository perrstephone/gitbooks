# 🎡 Attractions

## The Bean

* free
* Formally, Cloud Gate

## Chinatown

* Great restaurants and cool spots to shop

## Navy Pier

* Ferris Wheel
* Shopping
* Hanging out by the lake
* Lots of food (Harry Carey’s, Giordano’s, McDonald’s, Ben & Jerry’s, etc.)

## Sears Tower

* $25/person
* Big building
* Cool observatory at the top
* Generally has a bit of a line (45 min to 2 hours)

## Grant Park

* Free!
* Buckingham Fountain
*
  * Massive water fountain&#x20;
  * Good for pictures
* Big park

## Lakefront/Riverfront

* Free public walking paths on the river and lakefront
* During summer, some small vendors with cocktails and small snacks open up
* Generally very pricy for mid-tier food, but the views make it worth it

## Shopping in Gold Coast/Michigan Ave

* Luxury shopping district&#x20;
* Hermes, Prada, etc.
* See also, luxury consignment stores
*
  * The RealReal

## Wicker Park

* More chill neighborhood with boutique stores and plenty of good restaurants
* Thrift stores as well!

## Fulton Market

* area with a lot of resturants
