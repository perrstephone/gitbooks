## Sears Tower

* $25/person
* Big building
* Cool observatory at the top
* Generally has a bit of a line (45 min to 2 hours)

## 360 Observation Deck&#x20;

This is an observation deck on the 94th floor of the John Hancock Center offering an incredible view of the entire city. It also has a bar and a ride called TILT which is a window that you hold onto as it physically rotates 30 degrees out the building.&#x20;

Price: $30-$35 per person for just admission, $49-$55 for a ticket that includes admission, TILT, and a drink ticket.&#x20;
