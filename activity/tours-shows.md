# ⛴ Tours/Shows

## Chicago Crime Tour&#x20;

* Charter bus cruises around Chicago for a few hours and brings you to big crime locations in the city
* $40/person

## River/Lake Tour

* Ranges, but typically $30/person for a decent one
* Get on a boat and are told facts about the city
* Some of them go on the lake, which is also very fun
* Chicago Architecture Tour is a great one for this!
*
  * [https://www.architecture.org/tours/detail/chicago-architecture-foundation-center-river-cruise-aboard-chicago-s-first-lady/#tickets](https://www.architecture.org/tours/detail/chicago-architecture-foundation-center-river-cruise-aboard-chicago-s-first-lady/#tickets)&#x20;
