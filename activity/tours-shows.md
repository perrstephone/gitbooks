## Chicago Architecture Tour

Chicago has a fascinating architectural history, and the Chicago Architecture Foundation is here to tell you all about it. It's about 1.5 hours and because they use ticketmaster prices range from $20-$50. Make sure to get the Chicago Architecture Foundation tours, they do an incredible job. An additional $5 gets you admission to the Chicago Architecure Center.

[https://www.architecture.org/tours/detail/chicago-architecture-foundation-center-river-cruise-aboard-chicago-s-first-lady/#tickets](https://www.architecture.org/tours/detail/chicago-architecture-foundation-center-river-cruise-aboard-chicago-s-first-lady/#tickets)&#x20;
